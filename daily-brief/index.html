🇮🇳 India
India IT Services — Where the P/E Premium Is Justified
TCS trades at 31x P/E, Infosys at 24x, Wipro at 22x. The 9-point spread between TCS and Wipro directly reflects their EBIT margin differential (26% vs 17.5%). The market is not just paying for growth — it's paying for margin quality. When a company's EBIT margin is 8.5 percentage points above peers, that translates mechanically into lower WACC, higher FCF, and a higher DCF intrinsic value. The premium is real, not sentiment-driven.

🌍 Global
NVIDIA's EV/Revenue at 10x — Is It the New Normal?
NVIDIA now trades at 10x EV/Revenue — a number that would have been called speculative in 2021. What changed: gross margins hit 60%+, which is unprecedented for a hardware company. When a semiconductor business earns software-like margins, it deserves a software-like multiple. The key question in any NVIDIA DCF is not the near-term revenue CAGR (60% is consensus) — it's the deceleration assumption in years 6-10 that drives 60% of the intrinsic value.
EV/Revenue: 10x Gross Margin: 60%+ P/E: 40x WACC: 13.8%
🇮🇳 India
Zomato's EV/Revenue at 15x — What the Market is Pricing In
Global food delivery peers (DoorDash, Delivery Hero) trade at 2.5-3x EV/Revenue. Zomato trades at 15x. The 5x premium is not irrational if you accept three premises: India food delivery is structurally underpenetrated, Blinkit quick commerce will reach 12-15% EBIT margins at scale, and there's no structural competitor to Zomato + Swiggy in a two-player market. Reject any one of those premises and the 15x becomes hard to defend against the DCF intrinsic value.
EV/Rev: 15x Global peers: 2.5x WACC: 16.3% Rev growth Y1-5: 35%

The Analyst's Question of the Day
🎯 Think About This
When does a high P/E ratio signal confidence — and when does it signal danger?
A 50x P/E is fine if the company is growing earnings at 25% annually (PEG ratio = 2x). It's dangerous if growth is 8% (PEG = 6.25x). The DCF version of this question: when WACC is 12% and terminal growth is 5%, a company needs a 7% spread minimum to create value. If it only generates a 4% spread, even a low P/E stock is destroying value. Always ask: what earnings growth rate does this P/E imply? Is that growth rate realistic? 📐 Concept of the Day: Terminal Value
Terminal value accounts for 60-80% of a typical DCF's total value. It's the single number that drives most analyst disagreements. Most DCFs use the Gordon Growth Model:
TV = FCF₁₀ × (1 + g) ÷ (WACC − g)
