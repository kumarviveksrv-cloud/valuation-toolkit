M&A Valuation Concepts — How Deals Get Priced
🏷️
Control Premium
Acquirers typically pay 20-40% above market price for control. This premium reflects synergies, strategic value, and the right to make operating decisions. In the EV Bridge, you'd add the control premium to the SOTP-derived equity value before calculating the offer price per share.
⚖️
Synergy-Adjusted EBITDA
When applying Comps multiples to an acquisition, use synergy-adjusted EBITDA, not standalone EBITDA. Cost synergies (overlapping functions) and revenue synergies (cross-sell) are added to the target's EBITDA. The multiple is then applied to this higher base.
Synergy EV = (EBITDA + Synergies) × Multiple
📊
LBO Entry Multiple
Private equity buys at 6-12x EV/EBITDA for most leveraged buyouts. The return comes from: EBITDA growth, multiple expansion at exit, and leverage paydown. A company bought at 8x can be sold at 10x after EBITDA doubles — generating 5x+ equity return even with no multiple expansion.
🎯
Football Field in M&A
In a real deal, the Football Field shows the acquirer's offer vs the target's stand-alone value. DCF gives intrinsic value. Comps gives peer market value. Precedent transactions give what similar deals paid. The football field sets the negotiation corridor — board approval requires the offer to be in the upper range.
