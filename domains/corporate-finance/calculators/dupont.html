DuPont: Breaking ROE Into Its Drivers

Two companies can have the same 20% ROE through completely different paths. One might have a 20% net margin with low asset turnover (FMCG). Another might have a 3% margin with massive asset turnover (retail). The DuPont framework forces you to understand which lever is creating returns — and whether it's sustainable.
