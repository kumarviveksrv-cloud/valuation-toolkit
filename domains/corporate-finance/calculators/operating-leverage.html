Operating Leverage: The Double-Edged Sword

High fixed costs are a bet on volume. If revenue rises, profit rises disproportionately — operating leverage amplifies the upside. But if revenue falls, those fixed costs don't disappear, and the same leverage destroys earnings fast. The Degree of Operating Leverage (DOL) measures exactly this amplification.

DOL = (Revenue − Variable Costs) ÷ EBIT [= Contribution Margin ÷ EBIT]
DFL = EBIT ÷ (EBIT − Interest) [Degree of Financial Leverage]
DTL = DOL × DFL [Degree of Total/Combined Leverage]

Interpretation: If DOL = 3×, a 10% revenue increase → ~30% EBIT increase
