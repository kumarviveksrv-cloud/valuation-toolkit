Cash Conversion Cycle: How Fast Does Cash Move?

The Cash Conversion Cycle (CCC) measures how many days it takes a company to convert its investments in inventory and other resources into cash flows from sales. A shorter CCC means faster cash generation. A negative CCC means the business collects from customers before it has to pay suppliers — customers are effectively financing the business.

CCC = DIO + DSO − DPO
DIO = (Inventory ÷ COGS) × 365 [Days Inventory Outstanding]
DSO = (Receivables ÷ Revenue) × 365 [Days Sales Outstanding]
DPO = (Payables ÷ COGS) × 365 [Days Payable Outstanding]
