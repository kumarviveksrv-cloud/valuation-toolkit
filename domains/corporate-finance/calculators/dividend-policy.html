Dividend Policy: The Three Choices

When a company generates earnings above what it needs to reinvest, it faces a fundamental question: what do you do with the cash? The three options — pay dividends, buy back shares, or retain for future investment — have different implications for shareholders, taxes, and signaling.

💵 Dividends

  • ✓ Signals confidence
  • ✓ Attracts income investors
  • ✓ Visible, reliable return
  • ✗ Taxed at 30% (India)
  • ✗ Sticky — hard to cut
  • ✗ Signals low growth

🔄 Buybacks

  • ✓ Tax-efficient (10% vs 30%)
  • ✓ Flexible — not sticky
  • ✓ EPS accretive
  • ✗ Signals limited growth
  • ✗ Board approval needed
  • ✗ May signal undervaluation

📈 Retention

  • ✓ Funds future growth
  • ✓ No tax leakage
  • ✓ Flexible deployment
  • ✗ Agency problem risk
  • ✗ Empire building risk
  • ✗ Requires ROIC > WACC
