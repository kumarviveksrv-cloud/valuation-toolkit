ROIC/EVA Framework —
Sector benchmarks for value creation and destruction
Sector Context
NOPAT = EBIT × (1 − Tax Rate)
Invested Capital = Equity + Interest-bearing Debt − Cash
─────────────────────────────────────────
ROIC = NOPAT ÷ Invested Capital
Spread = ROIC − WACC
─────────────────────────────────────────
EVA = NOPAT − (WACC × Invested Capital)
= Spread × Invested Capital
─────────────────────────────────────────
Positive Spread → Value Creation (grow capital)
Negative Spread → Value Destruction (shrink or fix)

—

Typical WACC
—

Spread Profile
—
ROIC − WACC
What the Spread Tells You
ROIC > WACC
+Spread
Every rupee of invested capital generates surplus returns. Grow aggressively. The stock deserves a premium to book value.
ROIC < WACC
−Spread
Capital is being destroyed. Each reinvestment cycle reduces shareholder wealth. Shrink the capital base or improve margins.
