Adjustment Type Reference

one-time One-Time Items

Genuinely non-recurring charges or gains. Restructuring costs, legal settlements, asset write-downs, transaction expenses. Must pass the "will it happen again in 3 years?" test.

run-rate Run-Rate Adjustments

Annualise benefits or costs that started mid-year. A headcount reduction in October must be grossed up to a full year. The underlying change must be verified — not assumed.

pro-forma Pro-Forma Adjustments

Reflect events that changed the business structure mid-year — an acquisition, a new factory, a divested unit. Require verifiable evidence (commissioning certificate, SPA closing date).

mgmt-addback Management Add-Backs

Items management wants excluded: ESOP, "non-cash" charges, litigation provisions. Buyers scrutinise these heavily — "non-cash" does not mean "not a real cost."
