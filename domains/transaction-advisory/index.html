What is Transaction Advisory?

🔍 Quality of Earnings

Reported EBITDA is a starting point, not the answer. TA advisors strip out one-time items, run-rate revenue, and management add-backs to arrive at the true economic earnings power of a business.

💰 Working Capital Peg

Deals are done on a cash-free, debt-free, normalised working capital basis. Getting the peg wrong by ₹5 Cr in a ₹100 Cr deal is a 5% pricing error that eats directly into the buyer's return.

📊 EBITDA Bridge

The bridge walks from statutory EBITDA to adjusted EBITDA — the number the deal actually prices on. Every line in the bridge is negotiated; sellers add back everything, buyers challenge everything.

✅ Deal Checklist

Structured frameworks for flagging revenue recognition issues, contingent liabilities, related-party transactions, and off-balance-sheet items before they become post-closing surprises.
