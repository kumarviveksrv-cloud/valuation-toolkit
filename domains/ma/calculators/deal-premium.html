M&A Premium Analysis
What is a Deal Premium?
When a company acquires another, it almost always pays more than the current market price. This excess payment is the deal premium — it compensates target shareholders for giving up their stake and reflects the acquirer's expectations of synergies and control.
Deal Premium = (Offer Price − Undisturbed Price) ÷ Undisturbed Price × 100

1-Day Premium = vs. price one day before announcement 1-Week Premium = vs. price one week before announcement 4-Week Premium = vs. price 4 weeks before announcement
The "undisturbed" price is chosen based on when market speculation began leaking. If the stock ran up on rumours, analysts use the 4-week price to avoid overstating the premium.
Control Premium
The value of being able to direct the company's strategy. Typically 20–30% even without synergies.
Synergy Premium
The portion of the premium that synergies justify. Accretive deals require synergies to cover this cost.
Leaked Deals
Stock often runs before announcement. Always check 30-day price action to pick the right undisturbed price.
Walk-Away Price
The minimum price target shareholders will accept. Roughly undisturbed price × (1 + floor premium).
