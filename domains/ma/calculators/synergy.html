Synergy Analysis
The Promise vs. the Reality
Synergies are the most cited — and most abused — rationale in M&A. They come in two flavours: revenue synergies (harder to achieve, often overstated) and cost synergies (more predictable, but still take 12–24 months to fully capture).
Revenue Synergies
Cross-selling, new geographies, pricing power, bundled products. Typically 2–5% of combined revenue. Take 2–4 years to fully materialise.
Cost Synergies
Headcount reduction, shared procurement, consolidated facilities, IT rationalisation. Often 5–15% of combined EBITDA. Faster to realise.
Integration Costs
One-time restructuring, severance, system integration, consultant fees. Typically 20–60% of annual synergies. Must be netted against PV.
Capture Rate Risk
McKinsey data: 60–70% of synergy targets are never achieved. Culture clashes and execution failures are the most common culprits.
PV of Synergies = Σ [Annual Synergy × Capture Rate × (1−Tax)] ÷ (1+WACC)^t

Payback Period = Integration Costs ÷ Annual After-Tax Synergies

Net Synergy Value = PV of Synergies − PV of Integration Costs
The IB rule of thumb: never pay more than 50% of the PV of synergies upfront as premium. The rest should accrue to the acquirer's shareholders.
