Sector M&A Dynamics
Acquirer Pro-Forma EPS = (Acquirer Net Income + Target Net Income + Synergies − Interest on Debt − Issuance Costs) ÷ Pro-Forma Shares
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Accretion / Dilution % = (Pro-Forma EPS − Standalone EPS) ÷ Standalone EPS × 100
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Accretive → Pro-Forma EPS > Standalone EPS (deal adds earnings per share)
Dilutive → Pro-Forma EPS < Standalone EPS (deal reduces earnings per share)
