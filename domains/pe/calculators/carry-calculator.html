💎 Carried Interest

How GPs Share in the Upside

Carried interest is the GP's performance fee — typically 20% of profits above a hurdle rate. But "above the hurdle" depends on the waterfall structure. Understanding whether a fund uses European or American mechanics fundamentally changes when — and how much — carry is paid.

1
Return of Capital (ROC)
100% of distributions go to LPs until they recover 100% of paid-in capital. No carry, no management fee offset — just return of the principal invested. In European waterfall, this is the whole-fund basis across all deals before carry kicks in.
2
Preferred Return / Hurdle
LPs receive a preferred return on their invested capital — typically 8% compounded. This "hurdle" must be cleared before the GP participates in profits. Accrues annually on unreturned capital. Some funds have a 0% hurdle (common in VC).
3
GP Catch-Up
After LPs clear the hurdle, the GP receives 100% of further distributions until it "catches up" to its carried interest percentage on total profits. Example: 80/20 carry → GP takes 100% until it has received 20% of all profits above ROC. Catch-up may be partial (50% to GP) in LP-friendly structures.
4
Carried Interest Split
Once GP is caught up, remaining profits split 80% LP / 20% GP (or per the LPA). GP carry = 20% of all profits above hurdle. For a $100M profit above hurdle: GP keeps $20M as carry, LPs take $80M. Carry flows to the carry pool, distributed to investment professionals per the team waterfall.

Carry Math

Total Profit = Gross Exit Proceeds – Total Capital Invested
Hurdle Amount = Capital × (1 + Hurdle Rate)^Years – Capital
Carry Base = Total Profit – Hurdle Amount (if positive)
GP Carry = Carry Rate × Carry Base e.g. 20% × Carry Base
LP Net Return = Total Profit – GP Carry – Mgmt Fees – Expenses
Sector Carry Benchmarks
Clawback Provisions If the GP receives carry on early profitable deals but later investments lose money (American waterfall), LPs can "claw back" carry already paid. GPs typically escrow 25–30% of carry in a clawback reserve. European waterfall eliminates this risk since carry isn't paid until LPs are fully returned — but it means GPs wait longer to receive carry on early winners.
