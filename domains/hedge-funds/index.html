Core Concepts
⚖️
Long/Short Equity
The dominant hedge fund strategy. Buy undervalued stocks (longs), short overvalued ones. Net exposure controls market beta; gross exposure drives total risk.
🌐
Global Macro
Top-down bets on interest rates, currencies, commodities, and indices. Uses futures and derivatives to express directional macro views across geographies.
🎲
Event-Driven
Trades around corporate events — mergers, spinoffs, bankruptcies, earnings. Merger arbitrage captures the spread between deal price and current market price.
🤖
Quantitative / Systematic
Factor models and statistical arbitrage. Mean-reversion, momentum, and pairs trading executed algorithmically. Low human discretion; high model dependency.
🔒
Market Neutral
Zero net market exposure — equal long and short book by dollar value or beta. Returns driven purely by stock selection alpha, not market direction.
📐
Risk Management
VaR, drawdown limits, position sizing, stop-losses, and liquidity management. Risk management separates sustainable funds from blow-ups.
