Sector Context
India-specific note — CCDs vs Equity
Most Indian VC deals use Compulsorily Convertible Debentures (CCDs) instead of direct equity for tax and regulatory efficiency. CCDs convert to equity at a pre-negotiated formula on a trigger (next round, IPO, or fixed date). The pre/post money math is the same — only the instrument differs. FDI guidelines under FEMA require CCDs for foreign investors in most sectors.
