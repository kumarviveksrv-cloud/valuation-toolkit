Understanding Burn Rate and Runway
<6
🚨 Danger Zone
Raise now or cut costs immediately. Investors will sense desperation — your leverage is gone.
6–12
⚠️ Watch Closely
Begin fundraise now (takes 3–6 months). Model bridge options. Identify quick wins on burn.
18+
✅ Healthy
Enough cushion to be selective in fundraising. Focus on reaching strong milestones first.
// Burn rate
grossBurn   = totalMonthlyExpenses
netBurn     = grossBurn - monthlyRevenue
runway      = cashInBank / netBurn    // months

// With revenue ramp (CAGR-adjusted)
// Iteratively solve: sum of (netBurn_month_i) = cashInBank
netBurn_m   = grossBurn - (ARR/12) × (1 + monthlyGrowthRate)^m

// Default scenario
