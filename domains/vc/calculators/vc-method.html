VC Method Valuation
Work backwards from your target exit value to compute the implied pre-money and required ownership stake.
The VC Method: Working Backwards from Exit
1
Estimate Terminal Value at Exit
Revenue multiple × projected revenue at exit year (T)
↓
2
Discount to Today (PV of Exit)
Terminal Value ÷ (1 + target IRR)^years
↓
3
Required Ownership % at Exit
Investment ÷ PV of Exit = minimum ownership needed today
↓
4
Implied Pre-Money Valuation
Investment × (1 − required ownership%) ÷ required ownership%
// Step 1: Exit value at year T
exitValue     = revenueAtExit × exitMultiple

// Step 2: Discount to present
pvOfExit      = exitValue / (1 + targetIRR)^holdPeriod

// Step 3: Required ownership at exit
reqOwnershipT = investment / pvOfExit

// Step 4: Adjust for dilution in future rounds
reqOwnership0 = reqOwnershipT / (1 - futureRoundDilution)

// Step 5: Implied pre-money valuation
preMoney      = investment × (1 - reqOwnership0) / reqOwnership0
