Anti-Dilution ClauseHow it worksIndian market standard
Broad-based Weighted Average (BBWA)Adjusts conversion price based on all new shares issued at lower price, weighted by total outstanding shares✓ Standard in Indian VC deals
Narrow-based Weighted Average (NBWA)Uses only preferred shares in denominator — more investor-friendly than BBWAOccasionally seen
Full RatchetConversion price drops to new lower round price — very founder-unfriendlyRare; only distressed situations
Pay-to-PlayInvestor must participate in down round to keep anti-dilution protectionSeen in some term sheets
Pro-Rata RightsRight to invest in future rounds to maintain ownership %Standard for lead investors; info right for angels
