SAFE Note Types and Conversion Mechanics
Valuation Cap SAFE Most common
Converts at the lower of: (a) the cap price or (b) the priced round price. Investor gets more shares if company is valued above the cap.
convPrice = min(capPrice, roundPrice)
Discount SAFE Angel rounds
Converts at a discount (typically 15–25%) to the Series A price. Simple but less investor-friendly in high-valuation scenarios.
convPrice = roundPrice × (1 - discount%)
Cap + Discount SAFE Hybrid
Has both a valuation cap AND a discount. Investor gets whichever is more beneficial — most investor-friendly structure.
convPrice = min(cap, round×(1-disc))
Post-Money SAFE Y Combinator
Cap is on the post-money valuation (including the SAFE itself). Ownership % at conversion is pre-agreed: investment ÷ cap.
ownership = investment / postMoneyCap

India context — CCDs vs SAFEs
India's FEMA and Companies Act don't recognize US-style SAFEs. Indian startups use Compulsorily Convertible Debentures (CCDs) — debt instruments that mandatorily convert to equity at a trigger event. The economic effect is identical to a SAFE, but CCDs are SEBI/RBI-compliant for foreign investment. Some Indian startups issuing to US investors use a GIFT City SAFE structure. The conversion math remains the same.
