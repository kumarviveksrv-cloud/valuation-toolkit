12 Concepts Every VC Analyst Must Know
📐
Pre-Money Valuation
What a startup is worth before new money comes in. The number founders negotiate hardest on.
Post-Money = Pre-Money + Investment
📊
Cap Table
The ledger of who owns what. Every round, ESOP grant, and SAFE conversion changes it. Track it obsessively.
Investor% = Investment ÷ Post-Money
🔭
VC Method
Work backwards from expected exit value, discount to present, then solve for required ownership to justify the investment.
PV = Exit ÷ (1 + IRR)^n
🏊
ESOP Pool Shuffle
Creating the pool pre-money dilutes founders, not investors. Investors always push for this structure.
Total = Founders ÷ (1 − ESOP%)
🛡️
Anti-Dilution (BBWA)
Broad-Based Weighted Average gives new shares if a down-round happens. Protects investors at the cost of founders.
NewCP = OldCP × (OS + SS) ÷ (OS + AS)
🔥
Burn Rate
Net cash consumed per month (gross spend minus revenue). The single most-tracked metric between rounds.
Net Burn = Gross Spend − Revenue
✈️
Runway
How many months until the bank account hits zero. Raise when you have 12+ months — not 3.
Runway = Cash ÷ Net Burn
⚡
Burn Multiple
How much net cash burned per dollar of net new ARR. Below 1.5x is great. Above 3x raises flags.
Burn Multiple = Net Burn ÷ Net New ARR
📝
SAFE Note
Simple Agreement for Future Equity. A convertible instrument with no interest, no maturity — just a cap and/or discount that determines conversion price at the next priced round.
ConvPrice = min(Cap ÷ Shares, RoundPrice)
🇮🇳
CCD (India)
Compulsorily Convertible Debentures. India's FEMA-compliant equivalent of a SAFE for foreign investors. Converts to equity at a priced round.
Must convert → equity within 10 years
📈
MOIC
Multiple on Invested Capital. The raw return multiple. A $1M investment returning $5M = 5x MOIC, regardless of time.
MOIC = Exit Value ÷ Investment
⏱️
IRR (VC Context)
Internal rate of return — annualises MOIC. A 5x in 3 years (73% IRR) beats a 10x in 10 years (26% IRR). Time kills returns.
MOIC = (1 + IRR)^n

Glossary — Key VC Terms
Pre-Money Valuation Formula
The agreed value of the company before a new investment round. Determines how much ownership the new investor gets.
Post-Money Valuation Formula
Pre-money + the new investment. Investor ownership% = Investment ÷ Post-Money.
SAFE Note
Simple Agreement for Future Equity. Converts to equity at the next priced round at a discounted price or valuation cap — whichever is lower.
CCD India
Compulsorily Convertible Debenture. India's FEMA-compliant equivalent of a SAFE. Required for foreign investment at seed stage.
Valuation Cap
The maximum valuation at which a SAFE converts to equity. Protects early investors if the company's next-round valuation is very high.
Discount Rate (SAFE)
Converts at a percentage below the next round price (e.g., 20% discount → convert at 80% of Series A price). Rewards early risk.
ESOP / Option Pool
Employee Stock Option Plan. Typically 10–15% set aside for hiring. Created pre-money at investor insistence (dilutes founders, not investors).
Cliff Vesting
No stock vests until the "cliff" date (typically 1 year). Standard 4-year vesting with 1-year cliff = 25% vests at month 12.
Anti-Dilution (BBWA) Formula
Broad-Based Weighted Average. Adjusts investor's conversion price downward in a down-round, issuing more shares to compensate.
Full Ratchet
Extreme anti-dilution: if any share is sold cheaper, investor gets fully repriced. Rare in normal deals. Punishes founders heavily.
Burn Rate
Net cash consumed per month. Gross burn minus monthly revenue. The metric VCs watch most closely between rounds.
Runway
Months until cash hits zero at current burn rate. "12 months of runway" is often the trigger to start a new fundraise.
Liquidation Preference
Investors get paid first (usually 1x investment) in an exit before common shareholders (founders/employees) receive anything.
Participating Preferred
Investor gets liquidation preference AND participates pro-rata in remaining proceeds. Founders often negotiate this out.
VC Method Formula
PV of Exit ÷ (1+IRR)^n = required ownership % → implied pre-money valuation. Works backwards from exit to entry.
Power Law Context
In any VC fund, 1–2 companies generate 80%+ of returns. Every other investment is optional noise. VCs bet on outliers.
