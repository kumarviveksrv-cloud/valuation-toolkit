How Analysts Screen for Stocks
Value Screen
Find stocks trading below intrinsic value. Low P/E, low EV/EBITDA relative to peers and history.
P/E < 15 · EV/EBITDA < 10 · PBV < 2
GARP Screen
Growth at a Reasonable Price. High earnings growth but not at an excessive valuation multiple.
PEG < 1 · ROE > 15% · Rev Growth > 12%
Quality Screen
High-quality compounders. Consistent ROE, low debt, strong FCF conversion, durable moat.
ROE > 20% · D/E < 0.5 · EBITDA margin > 20%
