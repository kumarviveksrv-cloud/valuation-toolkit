⚖️ How Analysts Build Peer Comparisons
Peer Group Selection
Peers are companies with similar business models, size, and growth profiles — not just the same sector. A mid-cap IT services firm compares to peers of similar revenue mix, not to TCS
Trading Multiples
The two core relative valuation metrics are P/E (equity value per ₹1 of earnings) and EV/EBITDA (total firm value per ₹1 of operating profit). Use forward (next 12-month) estimates, not trailing
Premium / Discount Formula
(Stock Multiple ÷ Peer Median Multiple − 1) × 100. If a stock trades at 32x vs peer median of 26x, it trades at a 23% premium. The question is whether that premium is justified
When Premium Is Justified
Higher ROE, faster growth, stronger balance sheet, better management track record, or a dominant market position can each justify a structural premium to peers. Premium is fair value when drivers are durable
Historical Spread Analysis
A stock that always traded at a 20% premium to peers but now trades at par is actually cheaper in relative terms — even if the absolute P/E looks the same. Track the spread over 3–5 years for context
