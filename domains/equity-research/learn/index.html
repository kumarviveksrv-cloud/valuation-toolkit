12 Key Concepts
📊
Price-to-Earnings (P/E)
Market price per share ÷ EPS. Tells you what investors pay for ₹1 of earnings. Sector-specific: IT trades at 25–30x, PSU banks at 8–12x.
🏢
EV/EBITDA
Enterprise Value ÷ EBITDA. Capital-structure neutral — better than P/E for comparing companies with different debt levels. Not used for banks.
💰
Return on Equity (ROE)
Net profit ÷ Shareholders' equity. Measures how efficiently a company generates profit from shareholder funds. Best-in-class NSE companies target 20%+.
📈
EPS (Earnings Per Share)
Net profit ÷ shares outstanding. The core output of every earnings model. Analysts forecast FY1, FY2, FY3 EPS and derive price targets from them.
🔢
PEG Ratio
P/E ÷ EPS growth rate. Accounts for growth: a 30x P/E with 30% EPS growth (PEG = 1.0x) can be cheaper than a 20x P/E with 10% growth (PEG = 2.0x).
🔄
Reverse DCF
Works backwards from current price to derive the implied growth rate. If that rate is unrealistically high, the stock is expensive — even if P/E looks reasonable.
⚖️
Risk-Reward Ratio
(Upside to target) ÷ (Downside to bear case). Analysts require at least 2:1 before publishing a Buy — ₹20 of potential gain for every ₹10 of risk.
🎯
Price Target
12-month forward equity value per share, derived from one or more valuation methods. Published with a Buy, Hold, or Sell rating and a thesis for why it's achievable.
🐂
Bull / Bear / Base Case
Three scenario outcomes probability-weighted to produce the risk-adjusted price target. Typical split: 25% bull, 55% base, 20% bear.
📉
EBITDA Margin
EBITDA ÷ Revenue. Measures operating profitability before capital structure and tax effects. Key lever in earnings models — a 1% margin change has outsized EPS impact.
🌀
Sector Rotation
Shifting portfolio weights across sectors as the macro cycle evolves. Financials and Industrials lead in expansion; defensives (FMCG, Utilities) outperform in contraction.
🔍
GARP Investing
Growth At a Reasonable Price — targeting companies with above-market growth but P/E multiples not yet stretched. PEG ratio is the core GARP screening metric.

Glossary — 16 Terms Every Equity Analyst Knows
CMP
Current Market Price — the live stock price used as the reference point for upside/downside calculations.
TP / PT
Target Price — the 12-month forward price an analyst expects the stock to reach, based on valuation models.
EPS
Earnings Per Share — net profit divided by total shares. The key metric price targets are built on.
P/E (Trailing)
Price divided by last 12 months' actual EPS. Useful for context but less used in target-setting than forward P/E.
Forward P/E
Price divided by estimated next 12-month EPS. The standard multiple used in price target calculations.
EV (Enterprise Value)
Market cap + net debt. Represents total firm value, which is more meaningful than equity value for capital-heavy companies.
EBITDA
Earnings Before Interest, Tax, Depreciation and Amortisation. The proxy for operating cash generation from the business.
NIM
Net Interest Margin — the banking equivalent of EBITDA margin. Spread between lending rate and cost of funds.
ROE
Return on Equity. Net profit as a % of shareholders' equity. The premier measure of capital efficiency.
CAGR
Compound Annual Growth Rate. The year-on-year growth rate used for revenue, EPS, and other forecasts over multi-year horizons.
Overweight / Underweight
Portfolio positioning relative to benchmark allocation. Overweight = hold more of a sector than the index; underweight = hold less.
Bull / Bear / Base Case
Three scenario outcomes: optimistic, pessimistic, and most-likely. Probability-weighted to produce the risk-adjusted price target.
Consensus
The average of all sell-side analysts' EPS estimates or price targets for a stock. Beating or missing consensus drives stock price moves.
Comps
Comparable companies analysis. Building a peer set and comparing trading multiples to assess relative valuation.
Upside / Downside
(Target Price − CMP) ÷ CMP × 100. Buy ratings typically imply ≥15% upside; Sell implies negative or near-zero return.
Margin of Safety
The gap between a stock's intrinsic value and its current market price. A buffer for being wrong — the more the better.
