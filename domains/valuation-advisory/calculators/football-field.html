What the Football Field actually is — and why every pitch book uses it
The Football Field puts every valuation method on the same axis. DCF gives an intrinsic value range. Trading Comps gives a market-anchored range. SOTP gives the segment-level range. EV Bridge gives the implied equity sensitivity. The 52-Week Range gives the factual market trading range. The chart doesn't tell you which is right — it tells you where they agree. The overlap zone is where the analyst has maximum conviction. If five different methods all say ₹1,200–₹1,600 is fair value and the stock is at ₹900, that is a strong buy signal. If they all disagree wildly, the valuation is uncertain by definition — not unclear analysis.
