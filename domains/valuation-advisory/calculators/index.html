DCF Calculator
Discounted Cash Flow · Intrinsic Value
Value a company on the cash flows it will generate over the next 10 years, discounted back to today. The foundational method of investment banking. If you understand one method, make it this one.
Key insight this teaches
Terminal value drives 70-80% of DCF output. The growth rate assumption you make for year 11+ matters more than any near-term forecast.

WACC Builder
Cost of Capital · Discount Rate
Build the discount rate you use in DCF — from scratch. CAPM for cost of equity, tax shield for cost of debt, and capital structure weights. Build WACC before you build DCF.
Key insight this teaches
TCS WACC is 12%. Tesla WACC is 15.4%. That 3.4% gap means every dollar of Tesla's future cash flow is worth less — which is why high-beta companies trade at lower P/E.

Trading Comps
Comparable Company Analysis · Relative Value
Value a company relative to what the market pays for comparable businesses. Apply peer median multiples (EV/EBITDA, EV/Revenue, P/E) to the target's financials. The most-used method in real IB work.
Key insight this teaches
Comps doesn't tell you what a business is worth — it tells you what the market currently pays for similar businesses. Those are two different questions.

EV Bridge
Enterprise Value → Equity Value → Share Price
Bridge from Enterprise Value to an implied share price — accounting for debt, cash, minority interest, and equity investments. The step every analyst must do after DCF or Comps, and the step most students get wrong.
Key insight this teaches
Cash is ADDED to equity value (not subtracted). Debt is SUBTRACTED. Getting this direction wrong is the single most common analyst error on Day 1 of an IB internship.

SOTP
Sum of the Parts · Segment Valuation
Value each business segment on its own peer multiple, then sum them up. Essential for conglomerates, holding companies, and any company with meaningfully different divisions. Reveals hidden value that blended multiples obscure.
Key insight this teaches
Jio at 18x EV/EBITDA + Reliance Retail at 35x + O2C at 8x = a total that's very different from the blended 11x the market uses. That gap is the thesis.

Football Field
Valuation Summary · Multi-Method Chart
Aggregate all five valuation methods into one chart — exactly as it appears in every IB pitch book. The zone where methods converge is your conviction zone. Build this after the other calculators, not before.
Key insight this teaches
When five independent methods point to the same price range, that's not coincidence — that's conviction. When they wildly disagree, that's a signal to question your assumptions, not your methods.

NAV
Net Asset Value · Balance Sheet Method
Restate a company's balance sheet at fair value and compute NAV per share. The gap between NAV and market price IS the franchise value — the brand, talent, and relationships that never appear on any balance sheet.
Key insight this teaches
HUL's NAV is ₹47/share. Its market price is ₹2,400. The ₹2,353 gap is Dove, Surf Excel, and Lux — 90 years of brand building that never touches the balance sheet.
