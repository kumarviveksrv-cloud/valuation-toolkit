Risk-Free Rate (%) ?Yield on a 10-year government bond. India: ~7.1% (G-Sec). US: ~4.5% (Treasury). This is what you earn with zero credit risk. Pre-filled
Beta (Levered) ?How much this stock moves vs the market. Beta > 1 = more volatile than index. This beta includes the effect of the company's debt on equity risk. Pre-filled
Equity Risk Premium (%) ?Extra return investors demand for holding equities vs bonds. India base: ~5.5%. US: ~5.5%. Higher in emerging markets due to structural uncertainty. Pre-filled
Country Risk Premium (%) ?Additional return required for investing in a specific country vs the US. India CRP ≈ 1.5%. Compensates for political, macro, and currency risks. Pre-filled
