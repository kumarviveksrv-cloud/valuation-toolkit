Core Concepts
🔄
Securitisation
Converting illiquid loan pools into tradeable securities. Originator sells pool to SPV, SPV issues PTCs (Pass Through Certificates) to investors. Removes assets from bank balance sheet, frees regulatory capital.
🏛️
Waterfall Priority
Senior tranche gets paid first (lowest risk, lowest yield). Mezzanine next. Equity/first-loss tranche absorbs losses first, receives residual cash flows. Sequential pay creates the "waterfall" structure.
🛡️
Credit Enhancement
Mechanisms protecting senior investors: overcollateralisation (OC), cash reserve accounts, excess spread, subordination, and third-party guarantees. Sized by rating agencies to support target credit ratings.
⚡
Prepayment Risk
Borrowers repay early, shortening the asset life. Measured via PSA speed (% of standard prepayment model). High PSA = fast prepayment = shorter WAL. Reinvestment risk for investors when rates are falling.
