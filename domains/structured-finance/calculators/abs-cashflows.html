PSA Prepayment Model

PSA 100% assumes 0.2% CPR in month 1, rising by 0.2% monthly until it stabilises at 6% CPR from month 30. PSA 150% = 1.5× standard; PSA 50% = slower prepayment. Higher PSA compresses WAL.

CPR to SMM Conversion

Conditional Prepayment Rate (CPR) is an annual figure. Single Monthly Mortality (SMM) = 1 − (1−CPR)^(1/12). Each month, the outstanding balance is reduced by SMM × beginning balance in addition to scheduled payments.

Weighted Average Life

WAL measures when, on average, principal is repaid — not the same as maturity. High PSA = shorter WAL. WAL is key for duration matching in ALM and for pricing ABS tranches against G-Sec benchmarks.
