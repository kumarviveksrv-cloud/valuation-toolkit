📐 What is PSA?

PSA (Public Securities Association) is the benchmark prepayment model for mortgage-backed and asset-backed securities. At 100 PSA, monthly CPR rises from 0.2% in month 1 to 6% by month 30, then holds constant. 150 PSA multiplies every CPR by 1.5; 50 PSA halves it.

🔄 CPR → SMM Conversion

CPR (Conditional Prepayment Rate) is annual; SMM (Single Monthly Mortality) is the monthly equivalent. The conversion: SMM = 1 − (1 − CPR)^(1/12). At 6% CPR (100 PSA steady state), SMM ≈ 0.5143%. This is applied to the beginning-of-month balance to derive prepayments.

⏱ Weighted Average Life

WAL measures the effective duration of an ABS pool — it is the time-weighted average of all principal cash flows. Higher PSA speeds compress WAL: a pool with 10-year WAM at 100 PSA might have 5-year WAL, while at 300 PSA that shrinks to 3 years. Investors use WAL to price extension and contraction risk.

🇮🇳 India PTC Prepayment Reality

Indian ABS pools (PTCs) use PSA as a reference, but actual prepayment depends on loan type. Gold loans (short WAM, high PSA speeds) prepay rapidly. Auto loans show moderate speeds. Home loans in India prepay faster than US mortgages due to top-up refinancing and lump-sum payments — typically 120–200 PSA for standard pools.
