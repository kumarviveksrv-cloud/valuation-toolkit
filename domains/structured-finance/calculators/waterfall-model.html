Sequential Pay Waterfall

Cash flows from the pool first pay senior tranche interest and principal. Only after senior is fully paid does mezzanine receive cash. Equity receives residual — earning higher yield but absorbing first losses.

India PTC Market

India's ABS market is dominated by Pass Through Certificates (PTCs). Vehicle loans, home loans, and microfinance pools are common collateral. SEBI and RBI regulations govern SPV structures and NBFC origination.

Credit Enhancement

The selected pool carries % credit enhancement — a combination of overcollateralisation (OC), cash reserve, and subordination protecting senior and mezz tranches against pool losses.

Prepayment Risk

PSA speed of % means borrowers repay faster than the standard benchmark. High PSA shortens weighted average life (WAL), reducing reinvestment duration for senior tranche holders.
